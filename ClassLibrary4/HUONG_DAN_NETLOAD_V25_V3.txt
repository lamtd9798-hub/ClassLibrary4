CLASSLIBRARY4 V25 - AI GOC MOI + THIET KE PCCC V3
=================================================

BAN V3 SUA LOI NOI DUNG TAB CHUA CHAY / ACMV / CTN BI TRANG.

1. DONG HOAN TOAN TAT CA CUA SO AUTOCAD.
2. GIAI NEN TOAN BO FILE ZIP VAO MOT THU MUC MOI, VI DU:
   C:\CAD_PLUGIN\ClassLibrary4_V25_V3
3. KHONG CHEP VAO BIN, OBJ; KHONG TRON CHUNG VOI V1, V2 HOAC DLL CU.
4. MO AUTOCAD, GO LENH NETLOAD.
5. CHI CHON FILE: ClassLibrary4.V25.dll
6. KHONG NETLOAD HAI FILE PHU:
   - ClassLibrary4.V25.DesignBridge.dll
   - ClassLibrary4.V25.FireDesignModule.dll
   Hai file nay phai nam cung thu muc va se duoc nap tu dong.

KIEM TRA SAU KHI NETLOAD
------------------------
- Tab 1 CHUA CHAY van co noi dung DUONG ONG / VAN / THIET BI / VE SHOP.
- Tab 2 ACMV va tab 3 CTN van giu nguyen noi dung V25 goc.
- Tab 7 EXCEL duoc doi thanh THIET KE.
- Tab THIET KE co doc dien tich, doc TEXT/BLOCK, goi y he thong,
  bo tri/dem dau phun, tinh luu luong, cot ap va chon DN ong.
- Phan AI moi cua V25 va bo chon he FF / CTN / ACMV / DIEN duoc giu nguyen.

LUU Y
-----
- AutoCAD khong the go DLL da NETLOAD ra khoi bo nho. Neu da nap ban cu,
  bat buoc dong AutoCAD va mo lai truoc khi thu ban V3.
- Khong xoa, doi ten hoac di chuyen rieng bat ky DLL nao trong thu muc nay.
- Neu Windows chan file: nhap chuot phai vao file ZIP truoc khi giai nen,
  chon Properties > Unblock (neu co), sau do giai nen lai.

CAU TRUC SUA LOI V3
-------------------
- ClassLibrary4.V25.dll: nen V25 goc moi nhat cua nguoi dung; chi them mot
  loi goi khoi tao tab THIET KE.
- ClassLibrary4.V25.DesignBridge.dll: chi thay noi dung tab EXCEL.
- ClassLibrary4.V25.FireDesignModule.dll: giao dien/thuat toan PCCC doc lap.
  Module nay KHONG chua ClassLibrary4.BOCTACHUI, nen khong tranh NameScope
  voi cac tab CHUA CHAY / ACMV / CTN.
